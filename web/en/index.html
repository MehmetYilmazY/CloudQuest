01 Fairness

If you thought it through,
it's right.

Most platforms compare your command as text. Put the flags in a different order, add a sudo — it says "wrong". You're not learning; you're memorizing what the editor wrote.

CloudQuest actually parses your command: it splits it into segments, tokenizes quote-aware, and checks your intent. The four lines beside this are all from the same task.

Task Make deploy.sh executable.
chmod +x deploy.sh PASSES
chmod deploy.sh +x different order PASSES
sudo chmod -v +x deploy.sh sudo + an extra flag PASSES
chmod deploy.sh no permission granted FAILS

05 Questions

Frequently asked.

I've never used a terminal — can I still start?
Yes — that's exactly what it was designed for. The first task is typing pwd. Nova explains what to type and why at every step, and after three wrong tries shows you the right command for free. If you like, take the Terminal 101 tour before you go in.
Do I need a real AWS account? Will I have to pay?
No. You type the commands in CloudQuest's own terminal; no credit card or cloud account is required. We do recommend trying what you learn on a real AWS Free Tier account, but it isn't mandatory.
How long until I'm a Cloud Engineer?
The curriculum is built around a 6-month programme: 8 chapters, 54 quests, 211 hands-on tasks. For someone putting in a few hours a week, 4-6 months is a reasonable range. Let's be honest: this game builds your muscle memory, and you'll need real projects alongside it to be interview-ready.
Can I lose my progress?
No. Your progress lives on the server and syncs across all your devices with your account. Verification also happens on the server — nobody can grant themselves a level from the console, so a badge you earned was genuinely earned.
What does it cover?
8 chapters: Terminal & Linux, Networking & Git, AWS Core, Infrastructure as Code, Containers, CI/CD, Observability, and Security & Cost. Plus 6 advanced labs: Network Ops, Scripting, Docker Deep Dive, Terraform Pro, Ansible and Kubernetes.
Is it paid?
It's free for now. You do need an account — so your progress can follow you between devices and be verified.
